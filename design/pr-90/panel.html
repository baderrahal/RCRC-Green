Step 1 as a tick list of plots, pull request 90

This is a mockup, not a screenshot. It is drawn by hand from DrawingSheetPanel.cs, PlotTickList.cs and PanelSteps.cs so the layout and the wording can be read before someone spends an install on it. It cannot show how Revit will really render the panel, how the WPF controls space themselves, or how the theme colours come out on a real monitor.

Everything in the panels below comes from the code: the words from PanelSteps and the panel file, the stems from SheetNumberRun.StemOf, and the counts from PlotTickList.

Step 1 stopped being one prefix with a From and a To. Every two letter prefix the model holds is a plot with a tick, several can be ticked at once, a ticked plot opens its own From and To over its sub plots, and every in-range sub plot is one line: its tick, its identifier and the number stem its sheets will take, so 010DM42A is readable here before step 4 builds it. The plots below are DM ticked whole, FP ticked with FP-2 unticked, and MM left alone. The marker dropdowns that used to sit here are deleted.

What the shut step says

With step 1 shut, its header carries the whole state on its own, the words from PanelSteps: 1  PLOTS   DM, FP, 5 of 6 sub plots ticked. Before anything is ticked it reads 1  PLOTS    160 in the model, none ticked, and the empty grid says Tick a plot in step 1. Its sub plots list themselves under it, and the grid follows.

Where the stem goes

The faint stem beside each sub plot is the middle of every sheet number step 4 builds for it: DM-42's two 010 sheets read 010DM42A and 010DM42B and its one 200 sheet reads 200DM42, from pull request 89. Nothing is set here any more, which is why the marker dropdowns, their status lines and the plot-markers.txt caption are gone from this step.
