The title block fills itself in and remembers what it was told

This is a mockup, not a screenshot. It is drawn by hand from DrawingSheetPanel.cs, TitleBlockSettings.cs and PanelMetrics.cs, and it cannot show how Revit will really render WPF: font metrics, the dockable pane's width and the theme's own chrome all differ.

What it is for is reading the wording and the order of the new lines before somebody spends an install on them. Nothing here has been through Revit.

Step 4, one described sheet, at the pane's docked width. Three lines are new: the one under the picker saying where the title block came from, the one at the top of the step for a settings line that could not be read, and nothing else moves.

The other three lines that line can read

Same place, same faint colour, one per state. The first is after the user has changed the picker once. The second is two ticked views whose settings disagree, where neither wins and both are named. The third is a view type neither file knows.

AR-PRX-Title_Block_A1 GA-SCHEMATIC, from your own settings.
These views disagree about the title block: (010) Overall Plan wants AR-PRX-Title_Block_A1 KEYPLAN, (200) General Arrangement Layout wants AR-PRX-Title_Block_A1 GA-SCHEMATIC. Pick the one this sheet should use and it is remembered for all of them.
The settings hold no title block for (600) KERBS SCHEDULE. Pick one and it is remembered for next time.
